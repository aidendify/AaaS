What is AaaS

Agents as a Service: a team of AI workers you rent, not build.

I'm building AaaS because most small business owners I talk to lose deals to slow replies and forgotten follow-ups — not to bad products. Instead of buying another tool you have to operate, you get ready-made AI agents that do the work: a front-desk agent that answers leads, a follow-up agent that chases them, a quotes agent that drafts proposals, and a Chief of Staff agent that coordinates them and asks you before anything important happens. You see everything on one board, and every contact's full history on one timeline.

Questions

Good to know

What does "Agents as a Service" mean?

You don't build or configure AI yourself. You get ready-made AI agents that do specific jobs — answering leads, following up, drafting quotes — and you pay for the work they do, like hiring a service.

Who is AaaS for?

Indian small business owners who get enquiries on WhatsApp and lose them to slow replies: private clinics (skin, aesthetic, hair, dental — for enquiries and appointments only), coaches and consultants, real estate brokers, interior designers and home services, coaching institutes, salons, gyms and studios. Agencies can white-label it for their clients.

Will the agents send things without asking me?

Not unless you allow it. AaaS is approvals-first: each workflow is set to ask every time, act within limits you set, or run automatically — and automatic sends have daily caps. Emails to your customers go out only through your own connected Gmail.

Which channels does it work with?

WhatsApp, SMS, Gmail, Google Calendar and a website chat widget, with a shared inbox for WhatsApp and SMS.

Can I white-label it for my clients?

Yes. Agencies get a workspace per client, their own branding, staff roles and usage summaries with markup for invoicing. Custom domains aren't automatic yet — we set them up with you.

How much does it cost?

Pricing is in INR and shared personally during early access. There are no online payments yet — early users are invoiced directly. Join the waitlist and we'll walk you through the plans.

Hosted or self-hosted — which should I pick?

Most businesses start hosted. If you have strict client-data rules, you can run AaaS on your own server instead.

Can I try it before my spot opens?

Yes. Open the live demo, create an account and chat with the agents. You can even plug in your own AI key. Your waitlist spot stays saved.
