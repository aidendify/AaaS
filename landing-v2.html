Meet your AI team

One goal in. The right agent on the job.

You are not buying a chatbot that only books a slot. You are hiring a small team that shares one memory of each contact, and stops before anything leaves the business.

Orchestrator

Chief of Staff

Say what you want in ordinary language. It plans the work, delegates each part with the other agents, and brings one answer back. It can also add an agent when you ask.

Inbox

Front desk

Answers WhatsApp and the website chat from your profile and FAQs, asks what the person wants, and offers call times. The default is a draft. Full auto is not available. If it is unsure, it hands the chat to you and does not send.

Pipeline

Follow-up

Drafts the polite nudge for a lead or a quote, on the day you described. You approve the send. If you later set the step to auto, a daily cap and an allowed-recipient list still apply.

Revenue

Quotes

Tracks a quote you have sent and the first real open, so the follow-up is timed. The draft waits for you. Link-preview bots are ignored.

Memory

Contact timeline

Messages, notes, stage changes and bookings on one timeline, plus email opens, link clicks and page visits. Opted-out contacts are not tracked.

Routines

Plain-English workflows

A sentence becomes a trigger and steps. Each step is ask, within limits, or auto. Anything that sends, posts or pays starts on ask, with a daily cap once you allow it.

Learning

A Chief of Staff that reads the week

About once a week it reads only your account — timeline, opens, clicks, workflow runs, approvals — and writes a few plain suggestions. Apply saves a draft, or a change that still needs a separate confirm. It does not send. An A/B test on an email step splits two subjects and bodies and counts opens and clicks. Copying the winner never sends, and it leaves your approval setting and caps alone.

Playbooks

Starters, not a public store

Three built-in playbooks — agency intake, digital campaigns, email nurture — plus any playbook you export and install. The file has no contacts and no secrets. Install writes only after you confirm, and the workflows stay drafts until you turn them on.

Orchestrator, Desk and Factory boards show what each agent is doing, what is done, and what is waiting on you.

How it works

Tell it. It plans. You approve. It learns.

1

Tell it in plain words

A goal to the Chief of Staff, or a sentence on the Workflows page. No flow-chart to draw first.

2

It plans and delegates

The Chief of Staff splits the job and asks the front desk, follow-up or quote agent to do their part.

3

You approve

Sends, posts and payments land in Approvals. Edit, reject, or allow. From the queue, including on your phone.

4

It learns

Weekly suggestions and email A/B tests. Applying a suggestion does not send. You still turn a workflow on.

Control and safety

Fast replies, with the brakes on.

The default is ask. Within limits and auto exist, and you turn them on after you have read the step. Daily caps stay on either way.

Nothing important goes out by surprise

Front-desk replies, workflow sends and inbox replies start as drafts. Email to a customer goes out only from the Gmail that account connected. A payment step records the step. It never charges a card. There is no online payment in the app.

Your data, your account

Each business is its own account. A bring-your-own AI key is encrypted (AES-256-GCM) and the screen shows the last four characters. You can run hosted, or self-host so the data stays on your machine. AaaS does not train models on your data.

DPDP-aware, not a certificate

The waitlist asks before we message you. Customer opt-out words stop further sends. Tracking skips opted-out contacts. Purpose stays narrow: run the inbox, the booking and the follow-up you approved. This is not a legal opinion and not a compliance badge. Clinics should check their own duties with an adviser.

Clinics: enquiries and appointments only

Timings, fees, location, slots. Not diagnosis, medicines, reports or complaints. Those are handed to a person, and the desk does not answer them.

Questions

Good to know before you join.

What does Agents as a Service mean?

You describe the work in plain words and a ready-made team does the prep: answering, following up, drafting a quote, keeping the timeline. You are not assembling a blank agent builder. You still approve anything that sends, posts or pays.

Will it message customers without asking me?

Not on the default. Front-desk replies, workflow sends and messages you type all wait in Approvals until you approve them. You can later set a step to auto within limits or auto. The front desk cannot be set to full auto. Automatic sends keep a daily cap, and only that contact’s own address or number. A payment step never charges anyone.

Is the first reply really under 60 seconds?

Under 60 seconds is a target for preparing the first draft, including outside office hours, when a WhatsApp or website-chat message arrives. With the default setting the visitor does not see that draft until you approve it. It is not a measured average from customers, and it is not a promise that a scheduled follow-up fires in the same minute. On the hosted app, scheduled workflows are picked up on a routine tick that is often once a day.

Does it work in WhatsApp, and who sets that up?

Yes, through the WhatsApp Cloud API, with credentials you paste. We do that with you in early access. It is not Meta Embedded Signup. You also need your own templates for messages outside the 24-hour window. WhatsApp QR is a separate beta: unofficial, owner-only, off by default, and it needs a server that stays running. It does not message customers.

Can I talk to the Chief on Telegram or Slack?

Yes. Paste a Telegram bot token from BotFather, or a Slack bot token and signing secret, on the Inbox page. Your own messages go to the Chief of Staff. You can optionally let customers reach the front desk. Those replies wait for approval, and the front desk cannot be set to full auto. There is no per-message fee from AaaS.

I run a clinic. What will it say to patients?

Enquiries and appointments: timings, fees, location, and a choice of slots from the hours you set. It will not give medical advice. Symptoms, reports, prescriptions and complaints are handed to your staff and are not answered by the desk. This page is not legal advice about the DPDP Act.
