What you get

Your brand on the door. Their data in its own room.

Workspaces

A login per client

Adding a client creates their own account. Passwords are not emailed. You share them. Creating a client does not send a welcome email.

Brand

Name, logo, colours

Save a name, an https logo or a small image, colours, and a support email. Sign-in and password-reset wording uses your agency name. Mail to a client’s contacts still sends only from the Gmail that client connected.

Domain

Your hostname, set up together

You can store a hostname such as app.youragency.com. Saving it does not buy the name, change DNS, or issue a certificate. We do that with you: a CNAME to the host that already serves the app, or your own TLS if you self-host.

Staff

Owner, admin, member

The owner sets branding and markup. An admin can add clients, members, limits and playbooks, open view-as, and download the usage summary. A member can look at the list, the usage, and the DNS steps.

View-as

Read-only, and audited

Owner or admin only. An audit line is written first. The session lasts about two hours, cannot send or change settings, and cannot open the agency page. A member cannot view-as. The “back to agency” bar stays on screen.

Invoice prep

Usage, with your markup

A CSV or a printable HTML page for a month. AI cost stays in USD, with the percent markup you set. Messages and leads stay in INR at the rates you set. They are not added into one pay total. The page says it is not a tax invoice. This app does not take the payment.

Playbooks

A playbook per client, after you confirm

Install writes only after confirm. The workflows stay drafts. Install does not turn them on and does not send, post or pay. The same three starters exist, and you can install a playbook you exported. Caps, if you set them, are monthly counts for that client: AI calls, AI cost, messages and leads. A full cap blocks the new call, message or lead.

What this is not

No silent billing, no automatic domain

There is no card checkout and no Razorpay charge in this build. There is no domain API. View-as cannot message the client’s customers. One agency cannot see another agency’s clients.
